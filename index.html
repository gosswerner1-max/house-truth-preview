What you get

The short list

Only the problems we found, room by room, each with a photo and a short note. Rooms where everything was fine are left out, so you can read it in minutes.

The full checklist

Every single thing we looked at, including what was fine and what we could not check and why, so you can see exactly what was covered.

How it works

  1. Email us the property address, the town and the date you need.
  2. We walk through the house and the grounds and photograph what we find.
  3. You receive both documents by email.

What we look at

Roof, gutters and downpipes

Roof covering and shape, gutters, downpipes and where the water goes, and flat roofs.

Walls, cracks and damp

Cracks, damp and water damage, and the ground and paving around the house.

Ceilings, floors and tiles

Ceilings, floors, wall and floor tiles, and missing grout.

Kitchens and bathrooms

Fittings, sealing, tiles and countertops.

Garage and the door to the house

The garage floor and doors, and whether the door into the house is a solid door.

Wooden and fibre-cement houses

Wood rot, leaning, rusty fixings and damp, including houses built on stilts.

Braai areas, fireplaces and lapas

Fire bricks, hearths and chimneys, and heat damage to the wood around a fireplace.

Granny flats and other buildings

A flat or lapa on the property is checked like a small house of its own.

What this is, and what it is not

  • We look at what can be seen and reached on the day. We do not open walls, dig or take anything apart.
  • We do not test appliances, electrics, plumbing or gas. An electrician, plumber or gas installer does that and issues the certificates.
  • We list what we find. We do not quote for repairs and we do not tell you who to hire.
  • This is not a certificate and not a guarantee.

This report is accurate as of the day of the inspection, for what was walked through and checked. Anything covered up or hidden by previous owners or tenants could not be seen, and no tools were used to look for hidden defects.

Questions

What is a pre-purchase house inspection?

It is a careful walk-through of a house before you buy it, to record its visible condition. We note the problems we find with photos and send you a written record.

Does it include testing the electrics, plumbing or appliances?

No. We only look at what can be seen. Testing and certificates for electrics, plumbing and gas are done by an electrician, a plumber or a gas installer.

What if something cannot be seen?

We mark it Could not check and write down the reason, such as no access or furniture in the way, so you know what was not covered.

Do you check wooden and fibre-cement houses?

Yes. Wooden and fibre-cement houses get their own checks, including wood rot, damp, leaning, rusty fixings and houses built on stilts.

Do you tell me what repairs will cost?

No. We list the problems we found. We do not quote for repairs and we do not recommend contractors.

What does voetstoots mean?

Voetstoots means a property is sold as it stands. A written record of its condition before you sign helps you know what you are buying.
